Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

WebPViewer ("the app") is developed and operated by INFOKOM, LLC. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user information.

Data we collect

None. WebPViewer does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, usage statistics, telemetry, or analytics data. The app runs entirely on your Mac and does not communicate with any servers.

File access

WebPViewer can read image files that you explicitly open or grant access to via the system folder picker. When you add a folder via "+ Add Folder…", macOS issues the app a security-scoped bookmark that is stored locally inside the app's sandbox container. The bookmark allows the app to re-read that folder on later launches and is never transmitted off the device.

Third-party services

The app itself does not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services.

If you contact us via the Support form, the form submission is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the message to our support email. FormSubmit is only used on the website and is not part of the app itself.

Crash reports

If you installed the app via TestFlight, Apple may send anonymized crash reports and basic device information to the developer per Apple's TestFlight terms. This does not apply to the App Store release.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from children.

Changes

If we make any changes to this policy, the updated version will be published at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact support@infokom.me.
