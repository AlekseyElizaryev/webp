FAQ

Does WebPViewer work on macOS Catalina (10.15)?

Yes. The minimum supported version is macOS 10.15. The app ships as a universal binary, so it runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

What image formats are supported?

WebP (lossy, lossless), PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, and HEIC.

Why are some of my folders empty in the sidebar?

WebPViewer runs in macOS App Sandbox. Pictures and Downloads work out of the box thanks to system entitlements. For any other folder, use "+ Add Folder…" at the bottom of the sidebar — the access you grant will be remembered.

Does WebPViewer collect any data?

No. The app runs entirely on your Mac and doesn't make any network requests. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I report a crash?

If the app crashed, macOS opens a crash reporter window — please copy the report text into the form above. If you installed via TestFlight, the report is also sent to us automatically.
